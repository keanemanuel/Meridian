Bug: applicants who submitted a preference for BOTH Thursday
(17 Sep) and Friday (18 Sep) — or who submitted a single specific
day — are having their preference incorrectly collapsed/displayed
as only "17" (Thursday), even when their actual submitted
preference was 18, or both days.

This affects the day/time preference column (restored in Session
G5) and likely the underlying ingest/parsing logic that reads the
day preference from the raw form/CSV data.

Investigate:
1. Check the ingest/validation code that parses the applicant's
   day preference from the raw CSV column (likely a free-text or
   multi-select field like "Thursday, 17 September; Friday, 18
   September" per earlier examples in this codebase, e.g.
   "Thu 17 Sep; Fri 18 Sep" format seen in the Applicants tab).
2. Confirm whether the parser correctly distinguishes and stores
   BOTH days when both are selected, a single specific day when
   only one is selected, and doesn't default/fall back to "17"
   when the actual value is "18" or "both."
3. Check date parsing logic specifically — a common bug here is
   date string matching that's too loose (e.g. matching on "Sep"
   or partial substring rather than the full day+date), or an
   off-by-one/wrong-index bug when splitting a "day1; day2"
   string into separate values.
4. Confirm the SAME bug isn't also present in the solver's
   availability logic (i.e. is the scheduler ALSO treating an
   applicant who said "only Friday" as available Thursday
   instead, which would be a much more serious correctness bug
   affecting actual scheduling, not just display).

Fix the parsing so day preferences are captured accurately per
applicant — 17-only, 18-only, or both — with no collapsing/
defaulting to 17. Add a test with the specific formats seen in
this codebase (e.g. an applicant preferring only "Fri 18 Sep",
one preferring only "Thu 17 Sep", and one preferring "Thu 17 Sep;
Fri 18 Sep") asserting each is captured distinctly and correctly.

Run pytest. If tests pass, commit and push.
